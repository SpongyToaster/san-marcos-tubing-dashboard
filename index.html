🛶 San Marcos River City Rules & Guidelines

  • 🚫 Can Ban: No disposable containers allowed on river.
  • 🥤 Reusables: Non-disposable insulated cups/bottles only.
  • 🗑️ Mesh Bags: 1 mesh trash bag required per tube stack.
  • 🍷 No Glass/Styrofoam: Strictly prohibited by city ordinance.
  • 🌿 Protect Texas Wild Rice: Do not walk on underwater grass patches.
  • 🔊 Music Limit: Keep music volume respectful to river wildlife.
