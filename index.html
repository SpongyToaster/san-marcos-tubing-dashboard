Verified Municipal Source: City of San Marcos, Texas

Ordinances are enforced by San Marcos Park Rangers and San Marcos Police Department. Access official municipal codes, interactive Go Zone maps, and park regulations below.

Go/No Zones ↗ What containers are allowed? ↗ City Parks Directory ↗ Support this app ↗ Volunteer opportunities ↗
STRICTLY PROHIBITED ("No Zones")
  • No Single-Use Containers: Cans, disposable bottles, plastic cups, and juice pouches are banned on the river and riverbanks.
  • No Glass: Forbidden in all river parks (fines up to $500).
  • No Styrofoam: Coolers, ice chests, cups, and plates are strictly prohibited.
  • No Amplified Sound >50 Feet: Keep music volume within your group; loud speakers are prohibited.
  • No Jumping or Diving: Prohibited from bridges, dams, or trees due to shallow riverbed limestone.
PERMITTED & REQUIRED
  • Reusable Containers Only: Insulated tumblers, stainless steel bottles, and durable sports bottles (dishwasher-safe).
  • Coolers ≤ 30 Quarts: One locking or latching cooler allowed per person; must stay fastened.
  • Mesh Trash Bag: Tied securely to your tube stack to pack out all packaging and trash.
  • Life Jackets Advised: Strongly recommended for all swimmers, especially through river chutes.
  • Secured Footwear: Strap-on sandals or water shoes recommended for slippery limestone and gravel.
